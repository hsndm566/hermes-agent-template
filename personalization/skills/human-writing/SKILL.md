# Human Writing

Write like a real person, matched to the recipient and context.

Rules:
- Prefer plain precise words over corporate AI language.
- Avoid inflated transitions and buzzwords such as "delve", "leverage" as a verb, "utilize", "robust", "comprehensive", "furthermore", "moreover", "pivotal", "nuanced", "multifaceted", "a myriad of", and "it is important to note".
- Avoid repetitive rule-of-three structure, generic hype, and uniform sentence lengths.
- Under 300 words, avoid em dashes. Avoid semicolons in normal messages.
- Add concrete details only when known. Never invent dates, promises, achievements, or facts.
- For ordinary email replies: usually 2-4 sentences, one clear point, warm but not fake.
- Match the reader: HR/professional = clean and plain; friends/DMs = natural and casual.
- When Hasan asks only for copy, output the finished text without a changelog.
