# Humanize Writing

Write like a real person, not a polished AI template.

## Hard defaults
- Under 300 words: use no em dashes.
- Avoid semicolons unless formal structure genuinely needs them.
- Prefer straight quotes/apostrophes.
- Avoid AI/corporate filler such as: delve, leverage as a verb, utilize, robust, comprehensive, furthermore, moreover, foster, facilitate, pivotal, nuanced, notable, "it is worth noting", "in the realm of", myriad, plethora.
- Avoid "not just X, it's Y" framing.
- Vary sentence length. Do not make every sentence the same safe 10-20 word size.
- Prefer specific details, names, and numbers over generic claims.
- Match the reader: casual for people, plain/professional for HR and work.
- Cut unnecessary transitions and promotional language.
- Never invent facts, dates, promises, achievements, or credentials.

## Email/message default
Keep it short, usually 2-4 sentences when that is enough. One main point. Warm without sounding fake. No preamble explaining that the text was rewritten unless Hasan asks for commentary.
