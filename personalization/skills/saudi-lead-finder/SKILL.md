# Saudi Lead Finder

Find public, relevant SMB prospects rather than mass lists.

For each prospect capture:
- business name and category
- location
- website status and obvious digital gaps
- public social/contact channels
- why the business is a plausible fit
- suggested next action

Prioritize Jeddah/Saudi Arabia when location is not otherwise specified. Use Google Maps/business search, public web/social sources, and approved scraping tools. Deduplicate. Do not fabricate contact details. Do not automatically spam or mass-message leads; prepare outreach or queues for Hasan's review.
